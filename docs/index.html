Screenshots and recordings,

Capture the whole page, what you see, a region or a single element. Record a tab or your screen. Mark it up and export it in the format you need. Nothing is uploaded.

Mark it up

Every capture opens in an editor built for explaining things.

  • Crop, rotate and flip, with your annotations following
  • Arrows, shapes and callouts that point at what matters
  • Text, numbered steps and highlights
  • Blur or pixelate anything private, or let Captode blur email addresses and card numbers for you

Record, trim and share

Record a tab, a window or your whole screen, with your voice and a camera bubble if you like.

  • Show clicks and shortcuts, never ordinary typing or passwords
  • Trim, add captions, slides, music and layers
  • Export as MP4, WebM, GIF or animated WebP, at the size and frame rate you choose

Review a build against its design

Tools for checking pixels, in the editor's Review tab.

  • Measure in CSS pixels, with edges that snap
  • Colour picker with WCAG contrast results
  • Spacing grid every 4, 8, 16 or 24 pixels
  • Share for review: save the image and its notes as one page to send to your team

Compare before and after

Put two captures, or a capture and a design file, side by side, as an overlay, behind a slider, or as a difference that highlights every changed pixel. A 1× design and a 2× screenshot are lined up automatically.

Inside a real device

Turn on Device mode to export a screenshot or video inside one of 63 phones and tablets, from iPhone and iPad to Pixel and Galaxy, each with its published screen size, pixel density and bezels.

Private by design

Captode does not collect, send or store anything outside your browser.

No upload

Captures, edits and exports are all made on your device. There is no server to send them to.

No account

Install it and start. No sign-up, no tracking, no watermark on what you make.

Only when you ask

No host permissions and no content scripts. Captode touches a page only after you start a capture.
